# /social-engage - Social Media Engagement

## Mục đích
Tối ưu hóa engagement trên social media và quản lý community hiệu quả. Workflow này cung cấp templates, strategies, và playbooks cho việc build và nurture community.

## Input cần cung cấp
- Platform chính (LinkedIn, Twitter, Facebook, Discord, TikTok)
- Brand voice guidelines
- Engagement goals (followers, engagement rate, community size)
- Current metrics (optional)

## Workflow

### Phase 1: Content Calendar Strategy

#### Best Posting Times
```
LinkedIn:
- Tue-Thu: 7-8am, 12pm, 5-6pm
- Best day: Wednesday

Twitter/X:
- Mon-Fri: 8am, 12pm, 5pm
- Best day: Tuesday-Wednesday

Facebook:
- Mon-Fri: 1-4pm
- Best day: Thursday-Friday

Instagram:
- Mon-Fri: 11am-1pm, 7-9pm
- Best day: Tuesday-Friday

TikTok:
- Tue-Thu: 7pm-9pm
- Best day: Tuesday-Thursday
```

#### Content Mix (80/20 Rule)
- 80% Value content (educate, entertain, inspire)
- 20% Promotional content (sell, CTA)

#### Weekly Themes
```
Monday: Motivation/Weekly goals
Tuesday: Tutorial/How-to
Wednesday: Industry insights
Thursday: Behind-the-scenes
Friday: Community highlights/Wins
Saturday: Casual/Personal
Sunday: Planning/Reflection
```

### Phase 2: Engagement Templates

#### Reply Templates

**Positive Comments:**
```
"Thank you so much for the kind words, [Name]! 🙏 
We're thrilled to hear that [specific mention]. 
What's been your favorite feature so far?"
```

**Questions:**
```
"Great question, [Name]! 
[Direct answer]
If you need more details, feel free to DM us or check out 
[link to resource]. Happy to help! 💪"
```

**Complaints/Issues:**
```
"Hi [Name], we're sorry to hear about this experience. 
We take this seriously and want to make it right.
Could you DM us with more details? 
Our team will prioritize your case."
```

**Feature Requests:**
```
"Love this idea, [Name]! 🎯 
We've added it to our feature request list.
Would you mind sharing more about your use case?
This helps us prioritize better!"
```

### Phase 3: Community Management

#### Discord/Slack Welcome Message
```
Hey [Name]! 👋 Welcome to [Community Name]!

🎯 Here's how to get started:
1. Introduce yourself in #introductions
2. Check out #resources for guides
3. Ask questions in #help

🎁 Pro tip: React to this message to get the [Role] role!

Looking forward to having you here! 🚀
```

#### Community Guidelines Template
```
🌟 Community Guidelines

✅ DO:
- Be respectful and inclusive
- Share knowledge and help others
- Give constructive feedback
- Use appropriate channels

❌ DON'T:
- Spam or self-promote excessively
- Share offensive content
- Engage in personal attacks
- Share confidential information

Breaking these rules may result in warnings or removal.
```

#### FAQ Compilation
```
Q: How do I get started?
A: [Answer with link to guide]

Q: What are the pricing options?
A: [Answer with link to pricing]

Q: How do I contact support?
A: [Answer with support channels]
```

### Phase 4: Influencer/Partnership Outreach

#### DM Template - Collaboration
```
Hi [Name]! 👋

I've been following your content on [topic] - 
especially loved your recent post about [specific content].

I'm [Your name] from [Company]. We're working on 
[project/product] that I think could resonate with your audience.

Would you be open to a quick chat about a potential collaboration?

No pressure either way - just thought there might be 
a genuine fit here!

Best,
[Your name]
```

#### Partnership Proposal
```
Subject: Partnership Opportunity - [Mutual Value Prop]

Hi [Name],

Quick intro: [1 sentence about you/company]

Why reaching out:
- [Specific reason related to their work]
- [Mutual benefit]

Proposal:
- [What you're offering]
- [What you're asking]

Next step: [Clear CTA - call, reply, etc.]

Thanks for your time!
[Your name]
```

### Phase 5: Output

1. **Social Media Calendar Template**
   - Weekly content plan
   - Platform-specific adaptations
   - Hashtag sets

2. **Response Template Library**
   - 20+ reply templates by scenario
   - Customizable for brand voice

3. **Community Playbook**
   - Welcome sequences
   - Moderation guidelines
   - Escalation procedures

4. **Engagement Metrics Dashboard**
   - KPIs to track
   - Benchmark targets
   - Reporting template
