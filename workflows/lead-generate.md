# /lead-generate - Thu Hút Leads

## Mục đích
Tạo chiến lược thu hút và nuôi dưỡng leads hiệu quả. Workflow này bao gồm lead magnets, email sequences, landing page copy, và lead scoring framework.

## Input cần cung cấp
- Product/Service description
- Target audience (ICP - Ideal Customer Profile)
- Current funnel metrics (nếu có)
- Goals (number of leads, conversion rate)

## Workflow

### Phase 1: Lead Magnet Creation

#### Lead Magnet Ideas (Pick 1-2)

**Educational:**
- Ebook/Guide (10-30 pages)
- Checklist/Cheatsheet
- Template/Spreadsheet
- Video course/Webinar
- Case study

**Interactive:**
- Quiz/Assessment
- Calculator/ROI tool
- Free trial
- Demo

**Community:**
- Private Discord/Slack
- Newsletter
- Mastermind group

#### Lead Magnet Copy Template
```
Headline: [Number] + [Benefit] + [Timeframe]
"5 Ways to Double Your Leads in 30 Days"

Subheadline: [What they'll learn/get]
"Download our proven playbook used by 500+ companies"

Bullet points (3-5):
✅ [Specific benefit 1]
✅ [Specific benefit 2]
✅ [Specific benefit 3]

CTA: [Action verb] + [What they get]
"Get Your Free Playbook"
```

### Phase 2: Landing Page Copy

#### Above the Fold
```
Headline: [Primary benefit, keyword-rich]
Subheadline: [How you deliver the benefit]
Visual: [Lead magnet mockup or demo]
Form: [Minimal fields - name, email]
CTA Button: [Action-oriented, specific]
```

#### Body Content
```
Problem Section:
"Are you struggling with [pain point]?"
- Pain point 1
- Pain point 2
- Pain point 3

Solution Section:
"[Product/Lead Magnet] helps you [benefit]"
- Feature → Benefit 1
- Feature → Benefit 2
- Feature → Benefit 3

Social Proof:
- Testimonials (2-3)
- Logos of companies
- Numbers (users, results)

FAQ Section:
- Common objections
- Risk reversals
```

#### Thank You Page
```
Headline: "You're in! 🎉"
Next steps:
1. Check your email for [lead magnet]
2. While you wait, [additional value]
3. Follow us on [social] for more tips

Bonus CTA: [Low-friction next action]
"Book a free strategy call"
```

### Phase 4: Lead Scoring Framework

#### Demographic Scoring (Fit)
```
Job Title:
- C-level/VP: +20
- Director/Manager: +15
- Individual contributor: +5

Company Size:
- Enterprise (1000+): +20
- Mid-market (100-999): +15
- SMB (<100): +10

Industry:
- Target industry: +15
- Adjacent industry: +10
- Other: +5
```

#### Behavioral Scoring (Interest)
```
Website:
- Pricing page visit: +20
- Case study view: +15
- Blog visit: +5

Email:
- Open: +5
- Click: +10
- Reply: +25

Content:
- Downloaded lead magnet: +15
- Attended webinar: +20
- Requested demo: +30
```

#### Lead Qualification
```
Cold Lead: 0-30 points → Nurture sequence
Warm Lead: 31-60 points → More targeted content
Hot Lead: 61-80 points → Sales follow-up
Sales Ready: 80+ points → Immediate outreach
```

### Phase 5: Output

1. **Lead Magnet Package**

2. **Email Sequence**
   - 6-8 email templates

3. **Lead Scoring Matrix**

4. **Conversion Optimization**
