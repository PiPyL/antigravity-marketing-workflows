# /influencer-collab - Influencer Marketing

## Mục đích
Quản lý toàn bộ quy trình influencer marketing từ tìm kiếm, outreach, collaboration đến đo lường ROI. Workflow này cung cấp templates, contracts, và tracking frameworks.

## Input cần cung cấp
- Campaign objective (awareness, engagement, sales)
- Product/Service to promote
- Budget range
- Target audience demographics
- Preferred platforms (Instagram, TikTok, YouTube, etc.)

## Workflow

### Phase 1: Influencer Discovery

#### Influencer Tier Classification
```
Tier 1 - Mega Influencers (1M+ followers):
→ Wide reach, lower engagement
→ Best for: Brand awareness, credibility
→ Cost: $10,000 - $100,000+ per post

Tier 2 - Macro Influencers (100K-1M):
→ Good reach, moderate engagement
→ Best for: Product launches, campaigns
→ Cost: $1,000 - $10,000 per post

Tier 3 - Micro Influencers (10K-100K):
→ Targeted reach, high engagement
→ Best for: Niche markets, authenticity
→ Cost: $100 - $1,000 per post

Tier 4 - Nano Influencers (1K-10K):
→ Hyper-targeted, highest engagement
→ Best for: Local, community-based
→ Cost: $50 - $200 or product gifting
```

#### Vetting Criteria Checklist
```
Relevance (40%):
□ Content aligns with brand values
□ Audience demographics match target
□ Industry/niche relevance
□ Aesthetic fit with brand

Reach (30%):
□ Follower count appropriate for tier
□ Consistent growth (not fake followers)
□ Active across multiple platforms

Engagement (20%):
□ Engagement rate above platform average
□ Quality of comments (not just emojis)
□ Genuine interactions with followers

Resonance (10%):
□ Previous brand collaborations successful
□ Content quality and consistency
□ Professional communication
```

#### Engagement Rate Benchmarks
| Platform | Poor | Average | Good | Excellent |
|----------|------|---------|------|-----------|
| Instagram | <1% | 1-3% | 3-6% | 6%+ |
| TikTok | <2% | 2-5% | 5-10% | 10%+ |
| YouTube | <1% | 1-3% | 3-5% | 5%+ |
| Twitter | <0.5% | 0.5-1% | 1-2% | 2%+ |

### Phase 2: Outreach Templates

#### Initial DM/Email
```
Subject: Collaboration Opportunity - [Brand Name] x [Influencer Name]

Hi [Name],

I've been following your content about [specific topic] 
and absolutely loved your recent post about [specific post].

I'm [Your Name] from [Brand]. We're [brief brand description]
and I think there's a great alignment with your audience.

Would you be open to exploring a collaboration?

I'd love to share more details if you're interested!

Best,
[Your Name]
[Title, Company]
```

#### Follow-up (No Response - 5-7 days later)
```
Hi [Name],

Just wanted to follow up on my previous message!

We're planning a campaign for [product/event] and 
think your audience would really resonate with it.

Would love to chat if you have 15 minutes this week.
No pressure if it's not a fit!

[Your Name]
```

### Phase 5: Performance Tracking

#### Tracking Metrics
```
Awareness Metrics:
- Impressions / Reach
- Follower growth during campaign
- Brand mention volume

Engagement Metrics:
- Likes, comments, shares, saves
- Engagement rate
- Video views / Watch time

Conversion Metrics:
- Link clicks
- Discount code usage
- Direct sales attributed
- App installs

ROI Calculation:
ROI = (Revenue - Cost) / Cost × 100

EMV (Earned Media Value):
EMV = Impressions × Industry CPM / 1000
```
